AI customer service for small businesses: answer customers 24/7

Customers ask about prices and availability at night. Reply the next morning and some of them have already ordered elsewhere. An AI assistant answers them after hours and calls you in when someone is ready to buy.

5-minute read · Updated October 2026

What an AI assistant can do for you

  • Answer the same questions 24/7: prices, options, opening hours, delivery, bookings.
  • Ask the right follow-up questions, like size, date or number of people.
  • When someone wants to order, pay or haggle, or asks something it can't answer, it alerts you right away with the chat so far.
  • Reply in the customer's language: English, Chinese and more.

What it shouldn't do: invent prices, promise discounts or take payments. A good assistant answers only from your information and hands anything uncertain to you.

What to prepare

  1. Your price list: what you sell, prices and options.
  2. Common questions: hours, address, delivery areas and fees, pick-up.
  3. Deposit and payment rules.
  4. The tone you want: friendly and short, or more formal.

No tidy price list? A photo of your menu or a few past customer chats is enough to start.

How to keep answers accurate

  • Let it answer only from your information; for anything else it says "let me check with the owner".
  • Keep replies short, one or two sentences, like a real shop assistant.
  • Prices, discounts and payment always go to you.
