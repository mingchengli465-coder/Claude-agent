07FAQ

You might be wondering

How do I pay?

Prices are in US dollars. 50% deposit to start, the rest on delivery, by Alipay, WeChat Pay or card.

Can it work on WeChat?

Yes: a WeChat Official Account or WeCom, as well as Telegram, WhatsApp and others, whichever your customers use. I don't recommend a personal WeChat account: WeChat's rules make it easy to get banned.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, AI usage, and updates to your price list and FAQ. Monthly, cancel any time.

What if I'm not happy with it?

Three rounds of small changes are free; bigger changes are discussed. If you're not happy, I'll redo it.

Can you rush it?

Yes. I'll confirm exactly how fast.

I'm not very technical. Can I still use Claude Code?

Yes. We do it together over screen share, step by step, and I show you how to use it afterwards.

Am I talking to a real person?

The chat in the corner is my AI assistant, always online. For quotes, orders and details, I reply personally.

Can you issue an invoice?

Sorry, I can't issue invoices.
